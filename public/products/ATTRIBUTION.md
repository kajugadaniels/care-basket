# Product image attribution

No product images are currently included. Products without an approved local image use a category icon.

When images are curated, list each included filename, original 400-pixel Open Food Facts image URL, product page, attribution (Open Food Facts contributors), and [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) license. Do not list images that are absent. The developer must review and download the original files without modifications.
